A native macOS port of the engine of Indiana Jones and the Infernal Machine.

A retail copy of the game is required. OpenIndy3D contains no game data.

Free software under the GNU Affero General Public License v3. The complete source is at github.com/Jenavieve-Rose/OpenIndy3D; the license and notices are in the app (Contents/Resources/LICENSE and NOTICE).

Built on infernal-machine-decomp, with fixes from OpenJones3D by smlu, lessons from OpenJKDF2, SDL 3 and zlib.
